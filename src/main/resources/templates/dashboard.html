Mesh Settlement Lab

Observe an encrypted payment packet move through an offline relay network, then settle exactly once when a bridge device reaches the backend.

Checking service
What this shows: deferred delivery, not instant offline settlement. Relay devices can carry encrypted packets, but the final debit and credit occur only when the backend receives a valid packet.

Payment journey

Each action below updates the live simulation and the ledger.

01 CREATEBuild payment intentSender, receiver, amount, nonce, and timestamp are prepared.
02 PROTECTEncrypt packetAES-GCM protects data; RSA-OAEP protects the one-time AES key.
03 RELAYGossip offlineVirtual devices copy the opaque packet with a decreasing hop limit.
04 SETTLEBridge uploadsThe backend checks freshness and idempotency before a transaction.

The sender packet starts on the matching virtual device.
